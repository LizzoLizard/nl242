Research result

Main claim of the preprint
NLbal(9) = 240

The maximum nonlinearity of a balanced nine-variable Boolean function is exactly 240.

A balanced Boolean function on nine variables takes each output value on exactly 256 of its 512 inputs. Nonlinearity measures its minimum Hamming distance from affine Boolean functions. The question addressed here is whether a balanced function can achieve nonlinearity 242, above the balanced construction at 240.

NL242 presents a computer-assisted exclusion of the candidate value 242 through spectral-phase constraints, quadratic rank laws, exact Walsh identities, coding-theoretic obstructions and finite computational certificates.

Proof and verification scope

The release reports 79 compiled Lean 4.15.0 modules without Mathlib, covering structural reductions and selected exclusions. Remaining finite computational obligations are handled by published exact verification procedures and replay packets. The large finite enumerations are not all formalized within Lean. The manuscript's conclusion depends on the correctness and completeness of both components.

This is a research preprint, not a claim of independent peer-review acceptance.
